# Screenshot shot list

Five screenshots, 1600 × 900 px, taken from the running app in preview mode
(`DEMO_MODE=1`) with the seeded 248-product sample store. Captures live in this
folder as `01-dashboard.png` … `05-self-audit.png`.

| # | File | Screen | What must be visible |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `01-dashboard.png` | Dashboard | The "what applies now, and the 2026 changes still to come" banner and countdown, the score (`passing / total`), the three summary cards, per-check counts and the failing-products list. The disclaimer line under the score must be legible. |
| 2 | `02-bulk-editor.png` | Bulk editor | The "Apply a value to selected rows" panel with a field and value chosen, a few rows ticked in the table, and the per-row check badges. |
| 3 | `03-product-page-declarations.png` | Storefront product page | The legal declarations block on a product page: "MRP, inclusive of all taxes", manufacturer, country of origin, net quantity, common name and the date. |
| 4 | `04-grievance-block.png` | Storefront footer | The grievance officer block: name, designation, email, phone and the 48-hour / one-month statement. |
| 5 | `05-self-audit.png` | Self-audit | Several of the 13 items with their yes/no radios and notes fields, and the "record of your answers, not a certificate" note. |

## Capture notes

- Screenshots 3 and 4 come from the storefront block templates rendered with the
  demo product and shop settings metafields. If a Shopify dev store with a Dawn,
  Horizon or Refresh theme is connected, capture those two from the real
  storefront instead — the layout is identical, and that is the version App
  Review expects.
- Use a light admin background and no browser chrome. Crop to the app frame.
- Do not add text overlays or badges to the screenshots; the listing rules
  forbid superimposed claims.
