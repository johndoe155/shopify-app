# Pricing

Every plan includes every feature. Plans differ only by how many products the
store has. Paid plans include a 7-day trial. Annual billing is available on paid
plans at **ten times the monthly price**.

| Plan | Monthly | Annual | Products | Features |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | — | up to 10 | Everything |
| Standard | $19/mo | $190/yr | up to 500 | Everything |
| Pro | $39/mo | $390/yr | up to 5,000 | Everything |
| Scale | $79/mo | $790/yr | unlimited | Everything |

"Everything" means: full catalogue scan, bulk editor with CSV import and export,
the three storefront blocks, the dark-pattern self-audit, evidence exports and
the checklist help pages.

## How the product limit works

- The scan covers the products your plan includes. If your catalogue is larger
  than the plan allows, the dashboard tells you which products are outside the
  plan and which plan covers your catalogue size.
- Editing (writes to product metafields) pauses at the limit. **Nothing is
  deleted.** Your data and your price history stay exactly as they are.
- Upgrading resumes immediately.

## Choosing a plan

The app selects a plan only after you confirm the charge in Shopify's payment
screen. It never upgrades you on its own and never charges without that
confirmation.

## Cancelling

Cancel the app subscription in Shopify admin (Settings → Apps and sales
channels). Your access continues until the end of the paid period. Uninstalling
stops access; a shop redaction request deletes the data.

## What Shopify deducts from those prices

The prices above are the gross amounts charged to the merchant. They are not what
the developer receives:

| Deduction | Rate | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shopify processing fee | 2.9% of each app charge | Deducted by Shopify from the app charge |
| Shopify revenue share | 15% of app revenue | Applies after USD 1,000,000 of lifetime app revenue; below that threshold the share is 0% |

Taxes are not included in the figures above: Shopify's billing applies tax to app
charges as a separate line, which is a Partner dashboard setting and not something
the app code controls.

**The prices are gross amounts, and the developer's own Indian GST and income-tax
obligations are separate from them.** GST on an app subscription (18% on a domestic
supply, or zero-rated where the export-of-services conditions in section 2(6) of
the IGST Act are met) and income tax on what the developer actually receives are
liabilities of the developer, worked out from the payouts and not from the list
price. `docs/LISTING_FACTS.md` section 3 sets out the research trail and the
evidence to keep; it is not tax advice.
